# ADR-NNN: Short title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Deciders:** who was involved

## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play - technical, organisational, financial? What constraints apply? Describe the problem without proposing a solution. A reader who disagrees with the decision should still agree that the problem was real.

## Decision

What was chosen. State it in the active voice: "We use X" rather than "It was decided that X would be used." Include the key details a reader would need to implement it.

## Consequences

### Positive

What becomes easier, cheaper, or more reliable.

### Negative

What becomes harder, more expensive, or riskier. This section is mandatory. An ADR without negative consequences has not been thought through.

### Neutral

What changes but is neither better nor worse. Often includes follow-up work this creates.
